The Cursor mark from the reference, sculpted as a single free-standing puffy clay object, like a soft squishy toy: exactly the reference's hexagonal slab with its deep three-armed opening cut clean through the face, the opening an upside-down Y whose stem runs from the centre down to the bottom vertex and whose two arms reach the upper-left and upper-right vertices, so the slab reads as three plump rounded facets joined by a thick hexagonal rim, the same proportions and angles as the reference. Soft charcoal ink clay (#2B2B2E, matte, the faintest warm lift on the top faces). It stands upright facing the viewer squarely, chunky like a cushion, the Y opening reading at a glance from far away.
