The Kimi mark from the reference, sculpted as a free-standing puffy clay object, like a soft squishy toy: exactly the reference's solid K-shaped mark (the mark itself, not a printed letter), its upright a thick rounded column and its two broad plump blades fused into it, with the same flat-cut ends, angles and proportions; and the reference's small blue speech bubble as a separate puffy clay blob floating just off the mark's upper right, its tiny tail pointing down-left, in Kimi blue #027AFF. The K-shaped mark is soft ink-black clay (#191919, matte, the faintest warm lift on its top faces), the bubble a bright matte blue. It stands upright facing the viewer squarely, thick and chunky, instantly reading as the Kimi mark.
