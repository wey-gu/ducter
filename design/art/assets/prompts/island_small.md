A small clay island seen from slightly above (three-quarter top view, the same camera as the reference pedestal), the little sibling of a larger island: a gently irregular rounded oval, wider than it is deep. The top is a flat bed of soft sage-mint clay grass with a slightly lumpy hand-pressed surface, ringed by a thick, puffy rim of warm toasted-sand clay that overlaps the grass edge, then a pillowy pale-cream underside with three soft lobes like a cloud of clay. One tiny rounded butter-yellow clay pebble and one tiny mint pebble sit on the rim at the back right. The top is otherwise EMPTY and flat (a device will stand on it later). Clearly hand-made clay with soft thumb marks, not plastic. No water, no trees, no houses, no people.
