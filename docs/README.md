# Ducter docs

Ducter is a personal AI that runs on your own Mac or server. You talk to one partner, in one
conversation. It hands the work to an AI agent you already use
([which ones](faq.md#which-ai-agents-does-it-work-with)), on the plan you already pay for,
and comes back when the work is done, with how it checked. It asks you only when a decision is
yours to make.

These pages are for using Ducter. If you want to build it or change it, start with the
[README](../README.md) and [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md) in the repository instead.

> **Ducter is an early preview.** It works end to end on a Mac and on a Linux server, and it
> still has rough edges. Where something isn't built yet, these pages say "coming soon".

## Using Ducter

- **[On hand](on-hand.md):** every task at a glance, what each one is doing now, finding one
  fast, and acting on several at once.
- **[Past tasks and ongoing work](past-tasks.md):** find any task you had before by a few words,
  a file or a day, name and star the ones you come back to, carry on with one, start a new task
  that knows what it found, and pause or resume what runs on its own.
- **[Done for you](done-for-you.md):** everything Ducter sent, paid, called, signed in to,
  changed or deleted for you, day by day, with who asked and where; and which accounts it looked at.
- **[Your devices](devices.md):** which computers Ducter works on, what each one can do, how it
  keeps working when you close the window, watching Ducter's browser or using it yourself, and
  Ducter's own phone for apps.
- **[Your partner and settings](settings.md):** rename your partner, choose what it thinks with
  and who does the tasks, how it talks, when it may come to you and what it remembers, and the
  app's own settings and menus.
- **[Accounts your partner can read](accounts.md):** your mail, calendar, Slack and more, in one
  tap if you use oo, how it tells you only what needs you, and what it never does with them.
- **[Your calendar](calendar.md):** what's on across every Google account, a free hour, and
  adding, moving or answering events, each behind one tap.
- **[Your to-dos in other apps](to-do-sources.md):** the GitHub issues assigned to you and the
  to-dos in your Obsidian vault, taken on when you say, and commented on or ticked off when done.
- **[Passwords & cards](passwords-and-cards.md):** save a sign-in, a card or your travel details once, so your
  partner can book and buy for you, filling them in without seeing them, with your Go on every
  payment.
- **[Pictures and files](pictures.md):** send a screenshot, a photo or any file, see what a task made, ask for a
  drawing, and look through every picture in the conversation.
- **[Tables, charts, lists and choices](tables-and-charts.md):** when a reply reads better as a
  table, a chart, a list to tick or a few answers to tap, and how it reads in your chat apps.
- **[Talk by voice](voice.md):** hold the microphone and speak instead of typing, have your
  partner's messages read aloud, and where the sound goes on each device.
- **[Waiting, keep going, routines](waiting-and-routines.md):** "tell me when…", "keep fixing
  until it works", and "every Monday…".
- **[Phone calls](calls.md):** let your partner ring a restaurant, a clinic or a shop for you, only
  after you say Call, in its voice or yours, through Tuwa or from your own number.
- **[Your phone](phone.md):** connect your iPhone, iPad or Android phone, get a nudge when
  something needs you, and keep your nights quiet.
- **[iMessage](imessage.md):** text your partner from Messages on your iPhone, through a Mac,
  and get its answers in the same thread, under its own name.
- **[Chat apps](chat-apps.md):** talk to your partner from Telegram, Slack or WeChat, in
  the same conversation, through a contact of your own.
- **[Privacy and safety](privacy-and-safety.md):** what Ducter never does by itself, what runs
  where, your AI plans, and memory.

## Words you'll see

| You'll see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Partner** | The one you talk to. It's called Ducter until you give it another name. |
| **Task** | Something big enough that your partner hands it off to work on by itself. Each one gets a card. |
| **On hand** | Every task at a glance, and what is waiting for you. |
| **Needs you** | A task or question that is waiting for your decision. |
| **Waiting** | A task that is waiting for something to happen, such as "Waiting until PR #1592 passes". It looks by itself and carries on when it does. |
| **Routine** | Work that comes back on its own: every Monday at 9, or whenever something happens. You say yes to each one once, and can pause it any time. |
| **Accounts it can read** | Your mail, calendar and other accounts your partner can look in when you ask, and, if you say yes, keep an eye on for you. Reading just happens; sending or changing anything waits for your Go. |
| **Go, Hold and Reply…** | Your answers to a decision. Go takes the suggested default, Hold says "not yet", Reply lets you say it in your own words. |
| **How I know** | The proof under a finished result: tests that ran, checks, the changes. |
| **Devices** | The computers (and phone) Ducter can use: **Awake**, **Asleep**, or **Can't reach**. |
| **Partner and Settings** | Partner is about the one you talk to (name, language, notifications, memory); Settings is about the app. |
| **Stop Ducter and Wake** | The one way to stop everything, and the way to start it again. |
