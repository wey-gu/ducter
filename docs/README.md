# Ducter docs

Hand it ten things, and hear back only what needs you. Ducter is a personal AI for people who
build things, on your own computers or servers: your Mac, a server you own, and any other
computer you add.

You talk to one partner, in one conversation, from your phone, your desk or a chat app. When you
build, it hands the work to as many AI agents as it needs
([which ones](faq.md#which-ai-agents-does-it-work-with)), side by side on the plans you already
pay for, and any task is yours to open and steer. Finished work comes back with how it was
checked. In between it keeps an eye on your mail and calendar and runs errands, and it asks you only when
a decision is yours to make. [How Ducter fits together](concepts.md) draws the whole picture.

These pages are for using Ducter. If you want to build it or change it, start with the
[README](../README.md) and [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md) in the repository instead.

> **Ducter is an early preview.** It works end to end on a Mac and on a Linux server, and it
> still has rough edges. Where something isn't built yet, these pages say "coming soon".

## Hand it work

- **[Tasks](on-hand.md):** every task at a glance, what each one is doing now, finding one
  fast, and acting on several at once.
- **[@ and / when you write](at-and-slash.md):** point at a task, a file, a partner or a PR with
  @, and use Ducter's actions, your skills or an agent's own commands with /.
- **[Waiting, keep going, routines](waiting-and-routines.md):** "tell me when…", "keep fixing
  until it works", and "every Monday…".
- **[Past tasks and ongoing work](past-tasks.md):** find any task you had before by a few words,
  a file or a day, get back a file or table Ducter made for you, name and star the ones you come
  back to, carry on with one, start a new task that knows what it found, and pause or resume what
  runs on its own.
- **[Bring past conversations in](import.md):** bring your Lody conversations into Past tasks from
  Settings, read only, or copy the conversations you had in another app with the small format any
  reader can print.
- **[Pictures and files](pictures.md):** send a screenshot, a photo or any file, share from
  another app on your phone, see what a task made, ask for a drawing, and look through every
  picture in the conversation.
- **[Tables, charts, lists and choices](tables-and-charts.md):** when a reply reads better as a
  table, a chart, a list to tick or a few answers to tap, and how it reads in your chat apps.
- **[Your own small apps](apps.md):** ask for a little app (a trip expense tracker, a habit
  tracker) and keep it on every device, change it by asking, and ask your partner from inside it.
- **[Talk by voice](voice.md):** hold the microphone and speak instead of typing, have your
  partner's messages read aloud, and where the sound goes on each device.

  after you say Call, in its voice or yours, through Tuwa or from your own number.

  WeChat, in the same conversation, through a contact of your own.
