# Your own small apps

Ask your partner for a little app and it makes one, right there in the conversation:

- "Make me a little app to keep this Beijing trip's costs, by day, with a total"
- "A habit tracker: reading, a run, no phone after 11. I want to tick days off"
- "给我做个小应用，记一下这周健身"

It answers in a line, and a card with the app's name and **Open** goes under it. Open it, and it's
yours: type into it, tick things, add costs. What you put in stays, and it's the same on your Mac,
in the browser and on your phone.

## Finding it again

Every app your partner made is in **Past tasks › Made for you** (the **Apps** chip shows only
apps). Or just ask: "open my trip expenses app".

## Changing it

Say what you want, in the conversation or in the line under the app ("Ask Ducter to change it…"):

- "Add a total at the top"
- "Make it green"
- "Set a 3,000 budget and show red when I'm over"

Your partner changes the app in place; if it's open, it refreshes and says what changed. Don't like
the change? **Undo the change** at the top of the app brings back the one before (up to ten back).

Your partner can also fill it in for you: "add today's taxi rides to my trip app".

## Asking your partner from inside an app

An app can have a button that asks your partner something ("What do I need for the expense
claim?"). The question shows in your conversation in a grey bubble on the left, under the app's
name (it's the app asking, not you), and the answer comes back
into the app. Your partner treats what an app asks as a question, not as you saying yes: anything
that goes out (sending an email, paying, posting) still waits for your own **Go** in the
conversation, whatever the app says.

## A board that keeps itself up to date

Some things your partner does again and again: reviewing the pull requests waiting on you, checking
train fares every morning, running a benchmark every night. After a few rounds that look the same,
your partner asks once: **Keep these on a board?** Say **Make a board** and you get a small app that
each round updates by itself, so there's nothing to read in the conversation between rounds. You can
also just ask: "make these into a board".

For pull request reviews it's the **Review board**: every pull request waiting on you, its CI, what
the review found, and whether the draft is waiting for your Go or already posted. **Read draft** opens
the draft itself; nothing goes to GitHub until you say Go. You'll also find it from **Past tasks ›
Ongoing**, on the review's card (**Open “Review board”**).

## What an app can't do

An app runs closed off, for your safety:

- It can't reach the internet, load anything from outside itself, or open other pages.
- It keeps its data only on your own Ducter, never in the browser.
- It can only talk to your partner, at most one question at a time and 20 an hour.
