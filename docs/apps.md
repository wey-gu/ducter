# Your own small apps

Ask your partner for a little app and it makes one, right there in the conversation:

- "Make me a little app to keep this Beijing trip's costs, by day, with a total"
- "A habit tracker: reading, a run, no phone after 11. I want to tick days off"
- "给我做个小应用，记一下这周健身"

It answers in a line, and a card with the app's name and **Open** goes under it. Open it, and it's
yours: type into it, tick things, add costs. What you put in stays, and it's the same on your Mac,
in the browser and on your phone.

If it's about something your partner already knows, it starts filled in. Ask for a trip expense app
while you're on a trip your partner booked, and it opens named for that trip with your train and hotel
already in it, each marked **From your bookings**; the first time you open it, a line at the top says
what your partner filled in. Add the rest as you go: the total stays at the top.

If there's nothing to fill in yet, it opens ready for your first one: the top says what it will show
("Spent this month: Nothing yet"), a faint outline shows where your entries will list, and the form
to add the first is already open. Once you've added one, the top shows the real number and the form
folds back into its button.

## Your apps

Every app your partner made for you lives in **Your apps**: the **Apps** mark at the top of the
screen (four little tiles), or ⌘K. Each tile shows a little picture of what the app is about (a
suitcase for a trip, a bottle for a baby's feeds), what's in the app right now, big ("2 costs, ¥1,698
in all"), and one line about what it's doing:

- **Ducter is on it…** while your partner changes it for you
- **“Check fares each morning” updates it again tomorrow 9:00** when a repeating task keeps it up to
  date; if a round didn't work, it says so, with **Try again now**
- **Something went wrong in it**, with **Ask Ducter to fix it**
- **It still has the old look**, for an app made before apps got their current look, with **Give it
  the new look**: your partner remakes it, filling in what it already knows, and keeps what you put in
- otherwise, when it last changed

Tap a tile to open it; the same line sits over the app when there's something to do. Its **⋯** lets
you:

- **Rename** it
- **Ask your partner to change it**
- **Undo the change** (when there was one)
- **Pin to the top**: pinned apps come first and sit at the top of your conversation, one tap away
- **Stop** a repeating task from updating it
- **Delete** it: you're asked first, and what it keeps goes with it. **Undo** brings it back.

You can also just ask: "where are my apps?" or "open my trip expenses app". Apps are also listed in
**Past tasks › Made for you**.

No apps yet? **Your apps** suggests a few to start with, your own coming trip first. Tap one and your
partner makes it.

## Changing it

Say what you want, in the conversation or in the line under the app ("Ask Ducter to change it…"),
in the browser, on your phone or in the app's own window on your Mac:

- "Add a total at the top"
- "Make it green"
- "Set a 3,000 budget and show red when I'm over"

Your partner changes the app in place; if it's open, it refreshes and says what changed. Don't like
the change? **Undo the change** at the top of the app brings back the one before (up to ten back).

Your partner can also fill it in for you: "add today's taxi rides to my trip app". Or delete it: "delete
my shopping list app" (its card has **Undo**).

## Asking your partner from inside an app

An app can have a button that asks your partner something ("What do I need for the expense
claim?"). The question shows in your conversation in a grey bubble on the left, under the app's
name (it's the app asking, not you), and the answer comes back
into the app. Your partner treats what an app asks as a question, not as you saying yes: anything
that goes out (sending an email, paying, posting) still waits for your own **Go** in the
conversation, whatever the app says.

## A board that keeps itself up to date

Some things your partner does again and again: reviewing the pull requests waiting on you, checking
train fares every morning, running a benchmark every night. After a few rounds that look the same,
your partner asks once: **Keep these on a board?** Say **Make a board** and you get a small app that
each round updates by itself, so there's nothing to read in the conversation between rounds. You can
also just ask: "make these into a board".

For pull request reviews it's the **Review board**: every pull request waiting on you, its CI, what
the review found, and whether the draft is waiting for your Go or already posted. **Read draft** opens
the draft itself; nothing goes to GitHub until you say Go. You'll also find it from **Past tasks ›
Ongoing**, on the review's card (**Open “Review board”**).

## What an app can't do

Only you can see your apps, and each is the same on every device. An app runs closed off, for your
safety:

- It can't reach the internet, load anything from outside itself, or open other pages.
- It keeps its data only on your own Ducter, never in the browser.
- It can only talk to your partner, at most one question at a time and 20 an hour.
