# @ and / when you write

Wherever you write to Ducter or to a task, two keys help.

## @: point at something

Type **@** and a list opens above what you're writing:

- **Tasks**: the ones on hand first; type a few words to find earlier ones too.
- **Files**: inside a task, the files in the folder it works in.
- **Looking after**: what Ducter looks after for you.
- **Partners**: your other partners.
- **PRs and issues**: the ones your tasks opened or started from.

Use the up and down arrow keys to choose, Return or Tab to pick, Esc to close, or just click. What you picked shows
in your words, and Ducter gets the real thing with them: the task's latest result, the PR's state.

- **To your partner**, "@that task check it in Safari too" goes to that task, even if you were
  just talking about another one.
- **To a task**, "@another task use its report format" gives it that task's result and what it
  changed.

In what you sent, each one you picked is a small chip. Click a task's chip to open it, or a PR's
to open its page.

## /: use something

Type **/** for what you can use.

**When you write to your partner:**

- **Ducter can**: New task, Look after, Remind me, Hand over and Find. New task, Hand over and Find
  open right away; Look after and Remind me go with your words.
- **Your skills**: the skills you set up for your AI agents, each with what it does. Ducter starts
  a task that uses the one you picked.

**In a task**, at the start of what you write:

- **This task**: Pause, Switch who does it, and New task from this step.
- **The agent's own commands**, with their own descriptions: `/compact`, `/model` and `/effort`,
  and on Claude Code everything it offers, your own commands and skills too. They reach the agent
  exactly as you type them.

Only commands that really reach the agent are listed. On Codex, its skills are, written `$name`;
for what its own screen commands do (like `/review` or `/diff`), use the task's workbench and review
in Ducter.

On a phone the lists open as a sheet over where you write.
