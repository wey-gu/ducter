# Autofill

Ask your partner to book a hotel, a flight or buy something, and it can fill in the forms for
you: your sign-in, your card, your name and passport number. It fills them into its own browser
without the AI ever seeing them, and it asks you before every payment.

Open **Settings › Autofill** on your Mac, phone, iPad or Android phone.

## What you can save

- **Sign-ins:** a website, your user name or email, and the password. A sign-in is only ever
  filled on its own website. The first time it's used there, your partner asks you once.
- **Cards:** the number, expiry and the name on the card. The security code (the 3 or 4 digits
  on the back) is never saved: you type it each time you pay.
- **Your details:** your name as on your ID, email, phone, seat and meal preferences, your
  passport, birthday, home address, known traveler number and loyalty numbers.
  - Your name, email, phone, seat and meal your partner can read, so it can choose a window seat
    or say whose name the booking is in.
  - Your passport, birthday, address and loyalty numbers it can't read. It only fills them in, and
    the first time it gives one to a website it asks you.

## Paying

When a task gets to a checkout, you get a card:

> **Pay on ryokan-kurashiki.jp?**
> Amount JPY 38,400 · For 1 night, Nov 20 · Merchant ryokan-kurashiki.jp (first payment here) ·
> Card Visa ••4242

Type your card's security code and tap **Pay**, or tap **Not now** and nothing is paid. The
shop's name comes from the page itself, not from the AI. After you tap Pay, the card is filled
only on that site, for that one task, within 15 minutes. If the total changes, you're asked again.

If nothing is saved, the task stops at the checkout or sign-in page and you finish it yourself.

## What nobody sees

- After you save a password or card number, it is never shown again: not to the AI, not in your
  conversation, not in Settings, and not to you. Settings shows the site and user name, or the
  card's last four. To change one, type the new one; to look one up, use your own password manager.
- If you paste a card number or password into the conversation, it's hidden on screen and never
  passed to a task. It has still reached the AI, so it's best to add it in Autofill instead.
- One-time codes, bank checks (3-D Secure) and passkeys are yours: a task opens that page and you
  finish it yourself, in a live view of Ducter's browser, while the AI can't see.

## Where it's kept

Everything is kept on the computer Ducter lives on, locked. On a Mac the key is in that Mac's
keychain; on a Linux server it's in a file only Ducter can read. It's never copied to your phone
or other computers. The bottom of the Autofill page says which.

## Where it was used

**Where it was used** lists every fill, every payment you said Go to, every OK and every time you
said no, with the site, the task and when. **Remove** deletes a saved thing at once.
