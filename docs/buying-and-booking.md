# Buying and booking

Say what you want, the way you'd ask a friend:

- "帮我订下周五去杭州的高铁，早上的，二等座"
- "Buy the three things in my cart"

Your partner gives the whole errand to one task. It works in Ducter's own browser on the computer
where you're signed in to that site: it searches, compares, picks what you'd pick (the seat, the
time, the budget you've told it about), fills in your name and phone, and goes all the way to the
checkout page. Then it stops.

## The card at the checkout

You get one card, **Ready to pay on {site}** (in Chinese, **到 {site} 的付款这一步了**):

- **What**: what it's buying, with the items when there are several;
- **Total**: the amount the checkout shows;
- **Where**: the site, as Ducter read it from the page itself;
- a picture of the checkout page as it is now.

Tap **Go to checkout** (**去付款**). The checkout page opens right there, on your phone, Mac or
browser, the same page the task left. It's your turn: pay the way you always do (your card, a
wallet, a code). The task can't see the page while you're on it. When you've paid, tap **I've
paid** (**付好了**); the task reads the confirmation and tells you the order number. **Not yet**
(**还没付**) closes the page and keeps the card for later; **Not now** (**先不买**) on the card leaves
the cart as it is.

**Ducter never presses pay.** That last tap is always yours: Ducter's own browser refuses any
press that pays or places an order. The one exception is a card you saved in
[Passwords & cards](passwords-and-cards.md) and told it to use: then it asks you on a payment card
first, and pays only after your Go.

## When it needs you on the way

Some sites ask for things only you can do: signing in, a "confirm it's you" check, a code sent to
your phone. The card says what (**Your step on {site}** / **{site} 这一步要你来**); **Do it** opens
the same page, you do that one step, tap **Done**, and the task carries on from there. The first
time a site needs you signed in, you sign in once and Ducter's browser stays signed in.

## Afterwards

Everything you bought this way is in [Done for you](done-for-you.md), under **Paid**: **Ordered on
{site}**, the amount, and **You paid yourself**, with the task it came from.
