# Phone calls

Your partner can make a phone call for you: book a table, move an appointment, ask a shop
whether something is in stock. You say who and what for; it shows you exactly what will happen;
you tap **Call**; it tells you how it went.

The calls are made through **Tuwa** (tuwa.ai), a calling service with its own account. You sign in
to Tuwa once, and you pay Tuwa for the minutes, on Tuwa's own pages. Ducter never sees a card.

## Connect calling

1. Open **Settings › Accounts** and tap **Connect Tuwa** on the **Make calls** card. (Or tap
   **+ Calls** on the card of the computer Ducter lives on, under
   [Your devices](devices.md): it takes you to the same place and starts the same sign-in.)
2. A short code and a link appear (on a Mac, a QR code too). On your phone, open the link or scan the
   code, check that the code matches, and sign in with your Chekusu account (Tuwa's sign-in).
3. The card turns into **Calls · Tuwa: Ready to call**.

When you connect, Ducter sets a few things on your Tuwa account for you:

- a **daily limit** of $10 (Tuwa's own default is $50). Change it on the card: $5, $10, $20 or $50.
- Tuwa's **voice sampling** paused, and its **Friends** updates off: Ducter shares nothing about your
  life there.
- the phone agent's name: your partner's name.

If Tuwa ever asks you to sign in again, the card says so, with **Sign in again**.

## Ask for a call

Just say it: "帮我打给 Sushi Saito（+81 3-1234-5678），订明晚 7 点两位" or "Call the dentist and move my
Thursday cleaning". Give the number with its country code if you have it; if your partner needs
something more, it asks one short question.

You then see one card: **Call Sushi Saito?** with the number, what the call is for, what it may say
(the name to book under), what it won't say (your budget), the voice, the Tuwa number it calls
from, about how long and how much, and when. Tap **Call**, or **Not now**, or **Reply…** to change
something. Nothing is dialed before you tap **Call**, and there is never a "don't ask next time".

If it's too early or too late where they are, the card says so in their time ("It's 20:03 where
they are, so I set it for 9:00 their time"), the button reads **Call then**, and **Call now** sits
beside it when you asked for the call yourself. After 21:00 or before 8:00 their time it waits
whatever you say. Calls to your own number (one you say is yours, or the phone in Your details)
aren't held for anyone's hours.

Every call starts by saying it is an AI assistant calling for you. It never claims to be you.

## While it's on the line

**On hand** shows the call: **Calling…**, then **On the call** with a clock and **Hang up**.

If the other side asks something only you can answer ("is 7:30 all right?"), you get it at once as
**They're asking**, with **Yes / No** or a few choices and a field to say anything else. This one
comes through even in your quiet hours: someone is waiting on the line. If you can't answer within
about 90 seconds, the call says you'll call back.

## When it's done

Your partner tells you the outcome in one line, from what Tuwa reported. If Tuwa couldn't
confirm the result, it says so. On hand keeps the call for the day: tap it for the summary, what was
agreed, what was said (when Tuwa has it), how long it took and what it cost.

- **Got an automated message**: the call connected, but voicemail or a recording answered. If the
  number uses Google Voice's call screening, you'll see how to let calls through: answer and press
  1, or turn off **Screen calls** in Google Voice's settings.
- **No answer**: nobody picked up.
- **Didn't connect**: it was dialed but the line failed; Tuwa's own words follow when it gave any.
- **Didn't go through**: it was never placed (a card to save, no minutes left, today's limit, or
  Tuwa refused it).

## What it never does

- Call while you're in your quiet hours unless you just asked for it yourself, or call someone
  before 8:00 or after 21:00 their time. Between 9:00 and 20:00 their time is the usual window; you
  can choose **Call now** outside it, up to 21:00.
- Call emergency numbers. Call them yourself.
- Call countries Tuwa doesn't call. Tuwa's numbers reach the US, Canada, Japan, Korea, the UK,
  Germany, France, India, Brazil, Australia, New Zealand, Thailand, the Philippines, Singapore,
  Malaysia, the UAE, Nepal, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan. Mainland China isn't among them.
- Call anyone because an email or a web page said to.
- Go past your daily limit, or talk for more than about 15 minutes.
- Call someone who asked not to be called again. Tell your partner "don't call this number again"
  and it won't.

## Your own voice

Calls use your partner's voice. To use **your** voice instead:

1. On the **Calls · Tuwa** card, under **Your voice**, tap **Record on Tuwa**. Tuwa's own page opens:
   you agree there and read a short text aloud.
2. Back in Ducter, tap **I've recorded it**, then turn on **Call in my voice**.

Even in your voice, the call says it is your AI assistant speaking in your voice. Your voice is stored
by Tuwa's voice provider, in China. **Delete my voice** asks Tuwa to delete it. Only ever your own
voice: never a family member's, a friend's or anyone else's.
