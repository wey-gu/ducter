# How Ducter fits together

Ducter has very few parts, and you only ever talk to one of them. Here is the whole picture, from
you down to the computer the work runs on.

- **You**, on any device you like: your Mac, your iPhone, iPad or Android phone, a browser, or a
  chat app such as iMessage, WeChat, Feishu / Lark, Telegram or Slack.
  - You talk to **your partner**: one, with a name, in one conversation. It lives on your Mac or on
    a server you own, and it's the same partner wherever you open it.
    - Small things it does itself: an answer, a look at your calendar, a reply drafted.
    - Anything bigger becomes a **task**. Each task is done by an **AI agent** you're already
      signed in to (Claude Code, Codex, pi, DeepSeek Harness or Devin CLI, and Antigravity, Kimi
      Code CLI or OpenCode when you ask for them by name), and it can run several side by side.
      - Each task runs on one of **your devices**: the computer Ducter lives on, or another one you
        added. A coding task gets its own copy of your project and its own branch, so your own
        work stays as you left it.

Then it comes back up the same way. A finished task reports to your partner, and your partner
tells you, with **How I know** underneath. A decision that's yours comes to you with what it would
do, and waits. Everything else stays quiet.

## Why one partner, not a team

You could run a team of agents yourself: one for code, one for mail, one for research. Then you'd
be the one managing them: remembering who does what, checking on each, passing things between
them. With one partner you say what you want once. It works out the pieces, hands each one to as
many AI agents as it needs, and brings back only what needs you.

You don't lose sight of anything by it. **Tasks** shows every task, and opening one shows its AI
agent, every step and every change. You can steer it in your own words, or hand it to another AI
agent mid-way.

## What it can reach

Only what you turn on, and each one where you can see it:

- **The web**, in Ducter's own Chrome window ([Your devices](devices.md#browsing-the-web)).
- **Your Mac's apps**, with **Use the computer** ([Tasks in your Mac's apps](devices.md#tasks-in-your-macs-apps)).
- **A phone**: an Android phone of its own on your Mac ([Ducter's phone](devices.md#ducters-phone)),
  or your own Android phone, borrowed for a while ([Your phone](phone.md#let-tasks-borrow-your-android-phone)).
- **Phone calls**, after you tap Call ([Phone calls](calls.md)).
- **Your mail, calendar and other accounts** ([Accounts your partner can read](accounts.md)).
- **Your saved sign-ins and cards**, to book and buy up to the checkout, where you pay
  ([Buying and booking](buying-and-booking.md)).
- **Your memory**, shared with your other AI tools through Nowledge Mem, and **your other agents**,
  which can hand your partner work ([Your partner and settings](settings.md#talk-with-your-other-agents)).

Reading and drafting just happen. Anything that sends, pays, calls or can't be taken back waits for
your **Go**, and **[Done for you](done-for-you.md)** lists all of it afterwards.

## Words you'll see

| You'll see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Partner** | The one you talk to. It's called Ducter until you give it another name. |
| **Task** | Something big enough that your partner hands it off to work on by itself. Each one gets a card. |
| **AI agent** | The AI tool that does a task, such as Claude Code or Codex, signed in with your own account. |
| **Tasks** | Every task at a glance, and what is waiting for you. |
| **Needs you** | A task or question that is waiting for your decision. |
| **Waiting** | A task that is waiting for something to happen, such as "Waiting until PR #1592 passes". It looks by itself and carries on when it does. |
| **Routine** | Work that comes back on its own: every Monday at 9, or whenever something happens. You say yes to each one once, and can pause it any time. |
| **Accounts it can read** | Your mail, calendar and other accounts your partner can look in when you ask, and, if you say yes, keep an eye on for you. Reading just happens; sending or changing anything waits for your Go. |
| **Go, Hold and Reply…** | Your answers to a decision. Go takes the suggested default, Hold says "not yet", Reply lets you say it in your own words. |
| **Ask me later** | Puts a decision off to a time you pick. It leaves what needs you, nothing happens meanwhile, and it asks you again once at that time. |
| **How I know** | The proof under a finished result: tests that ran, checks, the changes. |
| **Merge into main** | How a finished coding task's changes reach your project. Only you choose it. |
| **Done for you** | Everything Ducter sent, paid, called or changed for you, day by day. |
| **Devices** | The computers (and phone) Ducter can use: **Awake**, **Asleep**, or **Can't reach**. |
| **Partner and Settings** | Partner is about the one you talk to (name, language, notifications, memory); Settings is about the app. |
| **Stop Ducter and Wake** | The one way to stop everything, and the way to start it again. |
