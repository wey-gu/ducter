## What each user felt

**The heavy multi-agent developer.** Fast and quiet. The ack names the action, the result is
one or two plain sentences, and the proof sits under it. The friction is all in honesty of
state: a merged result offered Merge again after a reconnect (and on the phone), and a
follow-up's new commits then showed as already merged (fixed, #1). The task's own words were
English and opened with "Branch ducter/tipcalc, commit 0210823" on On hand for a Chinese
request (fixed, #2). One task silently absorbed three different jobs (#3).

**The consumer (Grok bot / Muse) on the phone.** The phone is calm and readable; the
decision card with 好，最大余数法 / 先等等 / 回复… is clear, and Go lands in 3 s. What
breaks the spell: `ducter pair` printed a QR for `http://127.0.0.1:7801` that a real phone
can't open (fixed, #5); On hand said 在等 0 over a lane with one item (fixed, #9); a reminder
nobody asked for, written about "用户", sat in 在等 and in 接下来什么时候需要你 (fixed for new
ones, #4).

**The fresh user who has never heard of a subagent.** "Hi! I've never used anything like this.
What can you do for me?" got a warm, two-paragraph answer in 2.5 s, and "Remind me tomorrow at
9am to call my mom" a one-line confirmation. Before that, the desktop's first-run screen
showed `ws://127.0.0.1:…/ws` and "From source: run bun run up in the Ducter repo" (fixed,
#10), and the header kept saying "Nothing on hand" while On hand held tomorrow's reminder
(fixed, #8).

## Decisions this review needed (decided as D67, 2026-11-09)

1. **#3 Follow-ups vs. new tasks.** Recommendation: a follow-up goes to the old task only when
   it builds on that task's *unmerged* change; anything else opens a new task; and a task is
   retitled when a follow-up changes what it does. Default: as recommended.
2. **#12 Reversible asks carry a default.** Recommendation: an ask about how to do the work
   (reversible) always has a default and a time (e.g. 10 min); only effect-ladder asks
   (GQ1/GQ5) may wait forever. Default: as recommended.
3. **#15 Long paths in bubbles.** Recommendation: middle-ellipsis with tap to expand; the text
   sent is unchanged. Default: as recommended.
