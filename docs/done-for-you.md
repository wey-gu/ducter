# Done for you

Ducter does things in your name: it sends a mail after you say Go, pays with a card you saved,
calls a restaurant, signs in to a site, adds an event to your calendar. **替你做过的 / Done for you**
lists every one of them, day by day, so you can always see what happened out there.

## Opening it

- At the end of **Tasks**: **替你做过的 ›**.
- Under the partner's morning note: the same line.
- On the Mac: the menu **替你做过的… / Done for You…**, or ⌘⇧Y.
- Or just ask your partner: "你今天替我发了什么？", "Did you pay for anything today?". It answers
  from the same list.

## What you see

Each day starts with what happened that day, counted: how many things were sent, paid, called,
changed. Then one card per action, newest first:

- **What it was**, in plain words: Mailed Zhang Wei, Paid hotelokura.co.jp, Called Sushi Saito,
  Signed in to github.com, Added an event, Changed 14 files. Under it, the thing's own words when
  it has them (the message's start, the event's title).
- **Who asked**: you (in the conversation, or your own tap), a task by its name, or something
  that runs on its own (a routine).
- **The Go**: when you said Go, or that nobody answered and the card went ahead as it said it
  would, or that you allowed it there before.
- **Where**: which computer or phone, Ducter's browser, a phone line or your own number, or
  through which account.
- **Open the task** takes you to the task it belongs to; **See it** opens the result (the event,
  the pull request).
- **Undo** appears only where an undo truly exists: a task that changed files in a folder can put
  the folder back for a week. A message that was sent, a payment, a call or a merge can't be taken
  back, so they don't pretend to have an Undo.

**Looked at** lists the accounts Ducter read from (your mail, your calendar) and how many times.
It never shows what it read.

Use the filters (Sent, Paid, Calls, Sign-ins and fills, Changed, Deleted, Looked at) or type a
few words: who it went to, who was paid, which task. **Earlier** shows another week.

## A quiet day

Most days nothing goes out. Then the page says so, plainly: **Nothing sent, paid or called for
you today.** Under it you see what did happen, such as how many accounts it looked at.

## What isn't here

This lists what went through Ducter. If an AI agent runs a command on its own on your computer
(for example with its own approvals turned off), that command isn't listed here.
