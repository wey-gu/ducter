# On hand

**On hand** (手头的事) lists everything your partner is doing for you. Open it from the button
in the conversation's header, or press ⌘2.

## What you see

At the top is whatever **needs you**. Each one is the question itself, and you answer it right
there. Below that, the rest is grouped:

- **Working**: tasks under way.
- **Waiting**: tasks waiting for something, such as a check to pass, an email, or a computer to
  wake up.
- **Done today**: tasks finished today.

Each task is one row:

- **The first line** is its title. On the right is how long it has been working, or when it
  last changed.
- **The second line** says what it is doing right now, in words: "Running the tests",
  "Opened booking.com in Chrome". Once it's done, the line shows its result instead.

A task in a code folder also shows its branch, the lines it added and removed (**+42 −7**), and
**Draft PR** or **Merged** once you've done either from Ducter.

With one errand on hand, that's all there is: one row. More shows up as you use Ducter:

- **Find a task…** appears once you have ten things on hand.
- **By project** appears once your tasks are in two or more projects.

## On a Mac, an iPad with a keyboard, or a browser

- **j** and **k**, or the arrow keys, move through the list. **Enter** opens the selected task.
- **/** jumps to Find. **Esc** clears what you typed.
- **⌘O** opens any task by a few words of its title. If nothing matches, Enter starts a new task
  with those words.
- **Merge into main** or **Open draft PR** shows when you hover over a finished task with a
  branch. **Pause** shows when you hover over a task at work.
- On the full-screen On hand, **⌘-click** or **⇧-click** selects several tasks (or press **x**
  on a selected row). A bar at the bottom then offers what fits them: merge them, open draft
  PRs, pause them, or cancel them. Cancel asks you first.
- On a wide screen, a task opened from the full-screen On hand keeps the list beside it. **j**
  and **k** then move to the next task and the one before.

## On a phone

The list is the same and you tap a row to open it. Find appears there too once you have ten
things on hand.
