# Pictures and files

Your partner sees the pictures you send, the way a friend would in a messaging app. Ask about a
screenshot, send a photo of a whiteboard to work from, hand it a report, a log or a spreadsheet,
or ask it to draw something. When a task makes something worth seeing (a screenshot that shows it
works, a picture, a file), it shows up in the conversation.

## Send a picture

- **On your phone:** tap the paperclip left of where you type (**Add files or pictures**). Your
  phone's own menu opens: pick from your photos, take one, or choose a file. You can also paste a
  picture you copied.
- **On your Mac:** click the paperclip, paste (⌘V), or drag pictures and files onto the
  conversation. A file you copied in Finder pastes as that file.
- **Up to 10 at a time.** Each one starts going up the moment you pick it, under a small ring
  that ticks when it's there, so Send is instant. Type words with them, or send them alone. Up to
  nine show in your message at once, laid out in rows.
- **To a task:** open the task and send it pictures the same way, or drop them on the words when
  you start a **New task**. If an AI agent on the list can't see pictures, it says **Can't see
  images** there.

Before a picture leaves your phone, it is made smaller (2048 pixels on its long side) and
everything stored inside it is removed, including where it was taken.

If a picture doesn't go up (the connection dropped), your words and the picture stay where they
are, with **Didn't send** and **Retry**. Nothing you typed is lost.

## Share from another app

On your phone, anything you can share goes to your partner without saving it first: a page in
your browser, a photo, a post, a PDF. This works in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps.

1. Tap **Share** in that app and pick **Ducter** (the first time, you may need to find it under
   **More** and move it up the row).
2. Add a line if you like: "Summarize this", "Remind me next week". Leave it empty and your partner
   tells you what it got and offers what it could do with it.
3. With more than one partner, pick who it's for under **To** (it starts on the one you shared to
   last). Tap **Send**.

It arrives in that partner's conversation as one message: your line, then the link or what you
shared. Photos are made smaller and lose where they were taken, as always; up to 10 pictures and
files at a time, 50 MB each. Nothing is sent until you tap **Send**.

If your Ducter can't be reached right then, the sheet says **Sends when it's back**, and it
goes the next time Ducter opens on your phone. You need to have connected the Ducter app once;
sharing uses that connection.

## Send a file

Any file goes the same way as a picture: a PDF, a Word document, a spreadsheet, a log, some code,
a zip. It shows in your message as a card with its name and size and a small page marked with
what kind it is (PDF, DOCX, ZIP…). Where it can, the card shows a glimpse: a PDF's first page and
how many pages it has, the first lines of code or text, the first rows of a table. On your phone and in a browser, a video
also shows its first frame and length, and a voice memo or a song plays right in its card (in the
Mac app, coming soon). Tap the card to read the file
right there, like a file a task made (below).

- **Your partner reads it.** Ask about it in the same message ("summarise this", "what's wrong
  in this log?"). A short text it reads at once; a long one or a PDF it reads as it needs to.
- **A task gets it too.** When the work needs the file ("fix the bug in this script", "turn this
  table into a chart"), the task your partner starts has it, and works on its own copy.
- **Up to 50 MB each.** A bigger file isn't added, and Ducter says so: **That file is too big: up
  to 50 MB each**.
- **Nothing in it runs.** A program or a page you send is kept as it is and never opened as one:
  Ducter only shows its text, or offers **Save a copy**.
- A file is sent as it is: unlike a picture, nothing is made smaller or removed from it.

## Ask for a drawing

Say "draw…" (画一张…) and your partner hands it to an AI agent that can make pictures. Today that
is Codex, signed in on one of your computers. The picture comes back in the conversation when it's
ready. If none of your computers has Codex signed in, your partner tells you so.

## Look at pictures

- **Tap a picture** to open it big: it grows out of where it was. Pinch or double-tap to zoom
  (and drag to look around), swipe left and right through every picture in the conversation (or
  in the task) with the next one peeking in, and swipe down to close; it goes back into its place.
- **On a computer:** the arrows at the sides or ← and → move between pictures, a two-finger swipe
  does too, and pinching or ⌘ / Ctrl with the scroll wheel zooms where the pointer is.
- **Save**, **Share** and **Copy** are along the bottom. A picture a task made says **From**
  and the task's name: tap it to open that task.
- **On your Mac:** Space opens and closes a picture, ← and → move between them, a pinch or the
  scroll wheel zooms, ⌘C copies it, and you can drag it to Finder.

## Read a file a task made

A file a task made (a report, a table, a script) shows as one line with its name and size. Tap it
and it opens right there in Ducter:

- **A report** in Markdown is laid out like a document, with its headings, tables and lists.
  Its card already shows how it starts: the title and its first lines. **Show source** shows the
  text as it was written.
- **A wide table** in a report spreads to the whole width it has, and if it is wider still you
  scroll it sideways on its own: the first column (the names of the rows) stays where it is, and
  the edge fades where there is more to see.
- **A table** (CSV) shows as a table: its header row and first column stay while you scroll.
- **Fill the window** (the corners button at the top, ⌘Return / Ctrl+Enter, or a double click on the
  name) gives the file the whole window; do it again to go back. Ducter remembers it for that kind
  of file, so the next report opens the same way. On a phone a file already fills the screen.
- **Code and text** show with their colours and line numbers.
- **A PDF** shows its pages, one under the other; the top says how many there are.
- **Find** (the magnifier, or ⌘F / Ctrl+F) looks for words in a report, a table or code: every
  place they appear is marked, and the arrows (or Enter) go to the next one. On a Mac, a report
  or a table is searched in its text, and closing Find lays it out again.
- **Copy** copies the text, **Save a copy** keeps the whole file on your device (on a phone, your
  share sheet: save it to Files or send it to an app; on a Mac, your Downloads folder). **From**
  and the task's name opens the task.
- Close it with the ×, Esc, or by tapping outside it; on a phone, pull it down.

A very long file shows its first part and says so; Save a copy has all of it. Kinds Ducter can't
show yet (a Word or Excel file, a zip) say so, with **Save a copy**; on a Mac, **Open on this
Mac** opens it in its own app.

When your partner or a task names a file as a link ("Done: review.md"), tap it and it opens the
same way, even when the file is on the computer Ducter lives on or on another computer you lent
it, not on the device in your hand. Ducter opens only files in a task's own folders. When it
can't open one, it says why in one line: the file is gone, it isn't in a task's folders, it is
over 25 MB, or that computer can't be reached right now. On a Mac with Ducter on the same Mac, a
link to a file on that Mac opens in its own app, as before.

## Documents to send

Ask for a document the way you would ask a person: "make this week's progress into a one-page PDF
for my manager", "a 5-slide pitch deck", "put the visa papers into one PDF".

- **It comes back ready to send.** The task writes it in your language, with fonts that show it,
  and looks at every page before you see it. Ducter checks the pages too: text running off a page,
  a blank page, words left to fill in, the number of pages you asked for.
- **Flip through it right there.** Tap the card. A slide deck or a Word file made with a PDF of
  itself opens as its pages; **Save a copy** keeps the deck or the Word file, **Save the PDF** the PDF.
- **If something is still off,** **Every page checked** says what, one thing a line, under the
  result. Nothing there means the pages looked right.
- **To change it,** just say so ("make the title shorter", "drop the team slide"); the same file
  comes back changed.

## Good to know

- Pictures stay on the computer Ducter runs on. A picture is only reachable through a link that
  your own devices get from Ducter, and that link stops working after about a day: one that ends
  up somewhere else (a browser's history, a message) soon opens nothing. Your devices get fresh
  links by themselves.
- Files you send stay on the computer Ducter runs on, behind the same kind of link as pictures.
