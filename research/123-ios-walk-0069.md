# 123: The Settings panes changed since 0.0.67, walked on the iPhone (v0.0.69)

The Settings panes that changed between 0.0.67 and 0.0.69 (D329, D332, D336, D337) walked in the
iOS 26.3 Simulator in the real iPhone app (`apps/ios`, Debug, unsigned simulator build of `main` at
0.0.69), paired for real to demo cores, because desktop WebKit and Chromium shots miss what only
iOS does (emoji, safe areas, its own scrolling). Shots are in `.shots/ios-0069/` (not in git, D75);
names are cited below, `after-*` for the fixed ones.

  - `demo-memory.ts` with `DEMO_MEM=closed`: Memory and Agent messaging with the Mem app closed.
  - `demo-noted.ts`: D329's Noted lines in the conversation (撤销, waiting for Mem, Not kept).
- **Pairing and routes:** as research/110: `ducter pair --json` against the scratch data dir, then

- **Devices:** two simulators made for the walk (and deleted after): iPhone 17 (402 × 874 pt,
  Dynamic Island) and iPhone SE 3rd generation (375 × 667 pt, the smallest current screen). Light

- **Gestures:** cua-driver `drag` inside `run_script` (a read of the Simulator window first, or the
  drag is refused) for real touch scrolling; `click` at window pixels for taps.

## Findings, ranked, and what was done

1. **A sheet's content showed above its top edge (fixed in a second pass).** Scrolled to its end, a
   line of the page (Recent's last small line, the Partner page's "…keys hidden", the page title
   "other agents", a coin) was drawn up to ~10 pt above the sheet's rounded top, over the bar's own
   words (`17-peers-zh-dark-1`, `20-partner-en-17-dark-3`, `after-21-…-1`). Elsewhere in the scroll
   the content was clipped square at the top edge, not to the rounded corners. Five tries on the
   scrolling `.sheet` itself changed nothing: `clip-path: inset(0 round …)`, `-webkit-mask-image`
   (both forms), `contain: paint` with `isolation`, `overscroll-behavior: contain`, and no `rise`
   animation. INFERRED: WebKit's iOS scroll layer for a fixed scroller rounded only at the top.
   **The fix:** every sheet is now a frame (`.sheet-frame`: fixed, the radius, `overflow: hidden`,
   the rise and the drag) holding the grab and a body (`.sheet`) that scrolls inside it
   (`overflow-y: auto`, `-webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch`, `overscroll-behavior: contain`). The
   markup lives in one component, `apps/web/src/sheet-frame.tsx`, used by Settings and every
   `Sheet`, New task, the handset guide and the phone install sheet; `useSheetMotion` moves the
   frame and reads the body's scroll for drag-to-close. Re-walked on both phones, zh and en, light
   and dark, Partner, Agent messaging, Accounts and Memory scrolled to their ends: nothing above the
   edge, the corners clip, the grab stays put, drag-down from the grab still closes
   (`after-sheet-*`). The Mac app draws its own Settings (Go), so it is untouched. New task, the
   handset guide and the phone install sheet were checked by types and tests, not in the Simulator.
2. **The Partner page's invitation link lost its words (fixed).** At 375 zh it read **看.**; at 402
   en **Sh…**: the text button shrank in the row (`06-partner-zh-se-3`, `20-partner-en-17-dark-3` →
   `after-06-partner-zh-se-4`, `after-20-partner-en-17-dark-4`). It no longer shrinks.
3. **The own-number step's body was indented twice (fixed).** Inside its D289 block the body kept
   the older 52-px indent (`.set-page.pushed .own-body`), so it sat 52 pt past the text column and
   "上还没看到手 / 机" broke at 375 (`13-own-open-zh-se-mid` → `after-13-own-open-zh-se`). On the
   wide web too.
4. **Tap targets under 44 pt (fixed, D311's way: an invisible box, no visible change):** 试试看's
   copy button (32 × 32 → 44) and a Noted line's 撤销 / Undo and 忘掉 / Forget (20 tall → 44).
5. **怎么装 / How inside a setup step was unstyled (fixed).** The disclosure was styled only on a
   Settings row, so in Agent messaging's install step it showed WebKit's black triangle at body size
   with a ~24-pt target (`05-…-0`). It is now the same quiet dotted-underline line, 44 tall
   (`after-21-…-0`).
6. **"这台电脑不让 Ducter 自己装" on a phone (fixed, copy).** The install step names the home
   ("Ada's MacBook Air 上还缺…") and then says 这台电脑 / this computer, which on a phone is the
   phone (D330's sense). Now **Ducter 没法在那儿自己装 / Ducter can't add it there by itself**, on the
   web, the Mac table and the glossary line.
7. **试试看 at 375 (open, a design call).** The exchanges sit on the text column (D283's grid), so a
   bubble is ~195 pt wide and wraps to three lines in en (`04-peers-en-se-1`). Readable; in todo.
8. **Small:** the own-number steps showed a pressed band on 连线 after a drag
   (`13-own-open-zh-se-2`, the Simulator's pointer, not seen on a later pass); the notification
   offer's question breaks 这 / 台 (balanced heading, outside this walk). Twelve demos called the
   home `hel`, the author's own server, so the Mem-closed shots said "hel 上的 Nowledge Mem"; they
   now say `home-server` beside demo-core's Ada's MacBook Air (fixed in the second pass).

Guarded by four new tests in `apps/web/test/phone-fit.test.ts` (research/123 block).

## Implications for Ducter

- Scroll each sheet to its end on the phone before a release: the clip fault showed only there, and
  only in the real iOS web view. On iOS a scroller should not also be the rounded, fixed surface:
  put the shape on a frame and scroll a child.
- A rule written for one layout (`.set-page.pushed .own-body`) outlives the layout that replaced it
  (D289's blocks): a test that names both selectors keeps them from fighting.
- Text buttons in a flex row need `flex: none` wherever the row has a long sentence beside them.

**Summary:** the panes changed since 0.0.67 render without emoji, safe-area, dark-mode or join
faults on a 402-pt and a 375-pt iPhone. Five fixes in the first pass (a link shrunk to one character,
a step body indented twice, two sub-44 targets, an unstyled disclosure, one 这台电脑 on the phone);
in the second, every sheet became a rounded frame around a scrolling body, which ends the iOS fault
of content drawn above a sheet's top edge, and the demos stopped naming the author's server.
