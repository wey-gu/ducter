# 41: The Chinese name 「搭子」, a name-conflict check

Asked (2026-11-10, before v0.0.4): the author approved 「搭子」 as Ducter's Chinese product name,
with the brand form "Ducter 搭子". Before the pass that applies it: is the word free to use in
software (Nice classes 9 and 42), and who else uses it?

Tags: VERIFIED (read on the page here), DOCUMENTED (a secondary source says so), INFERRED.
Web only, read-only. No trademark lawyer was consulted and this is not legal advice.

## 1. Trademark registers: not reached

- CNIPA's own search (sbj.cnipa.gov.cn) cannot be scripted. The public mirrors refused or came
  back empty: Tianyancha answered HTTP 419 (`tianyancha.com/search/t400?key=搭子`), Qichacha reset
  the connection, Biaoju returned an empty shell. **Which class 9 and class 42 filings exist is
  not verified.**
- Searches for disputes, rejections or squatting over a "搭子" mark found nothing. That is an
  absence in search results, not proof that no filings exist.
- INFERRED: a 2023 top-ten buzzword is very likely filed across many classes, as happened with
  earlier hot words. CNIPA says it acts against bad-faith filings that ride a trend
  (DOCUMENTED, https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2023/4/26/art_3207_184727.html).

## 2. The word is generic

- 《咬文嚼字》 put 搭子 at No. 7 of its 2023 top ten buzzwords: a companion for any shared
  activity (DOCUMENTED, https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1701743613169859.html,
  https://www.chinanews.com.cn/sh/2023/12-04/10122252.shtml). China Daily gives the same
  definition and history: a Shanghainese word for card partners that has widened to a companion
  for anything (VERIFIED, https://language.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202312/13/WS65796632a31040ac301a7993.html).
- INFERRED: on its own, 搭子 has little distinctiveness. For an AI assistant it is close to
  descriptive ("a buddy"), so it would be hard to register alone and hard to defend. "Ducter 搭子"
  takes its distinctiveness from "Ducter".

## Implications for Ducter

- **Risk is high for marketing in China and medium for an open-source project without a China
  commercial launch.** No one can own the bare word, so a suit over the word alone is unlikely.
  The real risk is confusion and priority: since March 2026, "搭子" in AI and agents means Baidu's
  flagship agent product, which is in the same category as Ducter. A Chinese App Store or Android
  listing named "搭子" could draw a complaint.
- **Use the brand form "Ducter 搭子" wherever a name stands alone** (About, App Store-style
  names, a site's alternate name). Never use bare "搭子" as a store listing or registered name.
  Inside the zh UI, 搭子 as the in-sentence name of the product reads like the common noun it is,
  and that is the weakest place for a conflict.
- **Before any China store listing or filing**, a person runs the CNIPA search by hand
  (classes 9, 42, 35 and 38; the word 搭子 and the applicant 百度), and the author decides again
  with that result (`design/todo.md`).
- Ducter could not register 搭子 alone to defend it. If a defensible Chinese mark ever matters,
  it has to be a distinctive one.

**Summary.** 搭子 is a generic 2023 buzzword. Baidu has used it since March 2026 for 百度搭子, an
AI agent product in Ducter's category with millions of monthly users. The registers could not
be read, so filings are unverified. Recommendation: keep "Ducter 搭子" as the only standalone
brand form, keep 搭子 as the in-copy product word, and do a manual CNIPA search before any China
store listing.
